Solmar Heat — an open-world crime game built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Make GTA 6" bounty, $1,666 in USDC.

## The game

Rico Vega owes Marla Voss $40,000. Run **nine story missions** across Solmar Bay — taxi fares, a coupe heist, a police getaway, protection money, a ledger run, an underground street race, a bank vault job, a helicopter escape and the final payoff — to clear the debt.

- **Weapons** — pistol, shotgun and rifle with ammo and reloads (keys 1/2/3, R to reload).
- **Police** — a wanted system up to 5 stars, a police helicopter at high heat and roadblocks on the streets.
- **Garage** — spend mission money on top speed, acceleration, armor and paint (G); REP tiers unlock discounts and special paints.
- **Radio** — synthesized stations while you drive (C).
- **Day/night cycle** — the city runs from morning through dusk to night; street lamps and car headlights switch on after dark.
- **More rides** — steal motorcycles, sports cars and vans as well as sedans, each with its own speed and handling.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome.
